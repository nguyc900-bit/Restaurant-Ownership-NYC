Evidence 2

POPUP BAGELS

Started in Westport, Connecticut

Founded: 2020 by Adam Goldberg

Ownership / Investment: Backed by outside investors including Stripes, Tastemaker Capital, Habitat Partners, and Tiger Global

Ownership Change: 2022–2026 — PopUp Bagels raised multiple rounds of outside funding, including a $8M Series A led by Stripes in 2023, a $27M Series B led by Stripes in 2024, and a 2026 investment from Tiger Global at a reported $300M valuation. Exact ownership stakes were not disclosed.

Current Leadership: Tory Bartlett — CEO

Sources: Consumed, “Inside the Venture-Backed Bagel Boom” · DLA Piper · GlobeNewswire · Bloomberg Law
